# Q&A PREPARATION

## Most Likely Questions (Memorize These)

### "How do you get BIS data?"
"BIS publishes a public catalog at services.bis.gov.in. We scrape this for the MVP. For production, we propose a formal data partnership - BIS benefits from wider standards adoption."

### "What about accuracy/hallucinations?"
"We use RAG - Retrieval Augmented Generation - which grounds responses in actual standards data. Every recommendation has citations and relevance scores. Human review recommended for critical decisions."

### "How is this different from ChatGPT?"
"Three things: ChatGPT has outdated knowledge, can't traverse our standards graph for allied standards, and can't track real-time amendments. We're domain-specific, current, and relationship-aware."

### "Security for tender information?"
"Tender documents processed in memory, never stored. On-premise option available. All data in India. GIGW compliant, VAPT certified."

### "Multilingual support?"
"We integrate Bhashini - the government's own multilingual AI. All 22 scheduled languages supported."

### "GeM integration?"
"We're API-first. Our REST API can be called from GeM's tender workflow. We'd love to pilot post-hackathon."
